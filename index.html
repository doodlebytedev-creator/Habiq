What's inside

Everything a habit needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Build habits that fit your day, whether that's a glass of water eight times or a run three times a week.

01

Your day at a glance

Today groups habits by morning, afternoon and evening. One tap ticks a habit or adds a step.

02

Counts and goals

Track yes/no habits or amounts like glasses, pages and minutes, daily or a few times a week.

03

Streaks and history

See your streak, your best run and a heatmap of every day, so progress is easy to spot.

04

Reminders that work

Gentle notifications, or a full alarm for habits you really can't skip.

05

Home screen widgets

Log habits without opening the app: today's list, quick-log buttons, rings, heatmaps and more.

06

Looks the way you like

Calm themes in light and dark, in English, Hindi and Tamil.
