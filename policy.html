HabiQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: HabiQ keeps your habits on your phone. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data. There is no HabiQ account and no analytics. If you turn on Google Drive backup, your data goes to your own Google Drive, never to us.

What HabiQ stores

The habits you create, your daily check-ins, and your app settings (such as theme). All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. HabiQ has no user accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising. The app does not need an internet connection to work; it only goes online if you turn on Google Drive backup.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, HabiQ asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.

Backups and exports

When you choose Export backup or Export as spreadsheet, HabiQ creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. You decide where that file goes (for example Google Drive, your Files app, or email). Where you send it is governed by that service's own privacy policy. When you restore a backup, HabiQ only reads the file you pick.

Google Drive backup (optional)

You can choose to sign in with Google so HabiQ backs up your habits to your own Google Drive. It is off unless you turn it on.

  • What HabiQ accesses: only a private, hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). HabiQ cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files. It also reads your Google account's email address, only to show you which account the backup uses.
  • What it stores there: one backup file with your habits, check-ins and app settings, the same data as an exported backup.
  • How it's used: only to save your backup and to restore it when you ask, for example on a new phone. The data goes directly between your phone and Google; it never passes through or is stored on any HabiQ server, and we never see it.
  • Sharing: we do not share, sell or transfer this data to anyone, and do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Turning it off: in Settings → Backup and restore you can turn backup off, which removes HabiQ's access to your Google account. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the backup file itself, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose "Delete hidden app data" for HabiQ.

HabiQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual habits in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling HabiQ or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you used Google Drive backup, see above for how to delete the backup from your Drive.

Children

HabiQ does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If HabiQ adds features that handle data differently (for example optional paid features), we will update this page before those features are released and change the date above.
